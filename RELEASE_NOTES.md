## Overview

The Body Tracker now works as FitNotes's does: one screen with **Track**, **History** and **Graph** tabs for all your
measurements. Measurements and the calendar's chosen day are brought into line with FitNotes too. These were compared
against FitNotes screenshots that earlier comparisons had missed.

There are no database changes. Everything you've logged is kept as it was.

## Improved

- **Body Tracker** (from the day log's ⋮ menu) has FitNotes's three tabs:
  - **Track** lists every measurement with how long ago it was logged ("4 hours ago", "1 month ago"), its latest
    value and the change since the value before. One with nothing logged reads "Tap to record a value". Tap a
    measurement to log or edit a value.
  - **History** shows every value, newest day first, under a band for each day, with its time and change. Choose
    **All** or one measurement at the top.
  - **Graph** shows one measurement, picked from a dropdown, with its range, trend and goal options.
  The pencil opens **Measurements**; reordering is in the ⋮ menu.
- **Measurements** reads as FitNotes's: "Tap a measurement below to edit it or set a goal. Enable a measurement for
  tracking by hitting the checkbox." Each row shows the name, its unit in full (for example "Milligrams (mg)"), its
  goal, and a checkbox. Tap a row to set its goal.
- **The calendar's chosen day** lists the body values as rows, then each exercise under its name in capitals with its
  sets in columns, as FitNotes shows it.

## Known limitations

- Settings keeps its grouped pages for now; matching FitNotes's single Settings list is tracked separately.
- The day log's ⋮ menu keeps FitLens's extra actions, and workout comments stay on the day itself rather than in the
  menu, as decided earlier.
