## Overview

This update tidies the wording on the screens you use most during a workout. The exercise screen's History, Graph and
Records tabs, the exercise library, and the workout sheets (workout time, share, add, copy, move and delete) now take
their text from one place in the app, ready for future translations. Counts read properly throughout, for example
"1 set", "1 rep" and "Copied 1 set to today".

There's no database change. Everything you've logged is kept. Install it over the current app as usual.

## Improved

- **Exercise screen wording.** Every label, menu item, message and TalkBack description on the History, Graph and
  Records tabs, and in the graph's Compare, body overlay and share options, now comes from one place.
- **Set descriptions.** A set is described the same way everywhere, from the day log and calendar to shared workouts
  and the PDF report, and a single rep now reads "1 rep" rather than "1 reps".
- **Exercise library.** The category list, the workout switcher, the exercise form, Merge, the category sheets and
  the starter library all use the same consistent wording, with counts such as "Add 1 exercise" read correctly.
- **Workout sheets and tools.** Workout time, Share workout, Add or Replace workout, Create workout, and Copy, Move
  and Delete workout use the same wording, and their messages read properly for one set or many.

## Known limitations

- Graph names (such as "Estimated 1RM") and the record period names stay as they are for now. Graph names are also
  how pinned graphs are saved.
- Analysis, the exercise Stats and Goals tabs, the body tracker, the photo screens, the rest of the workout editor
  and the PDF report's own headings still keep their own wording and will move over as they're next updated.
- Shared screen transitions, the remaining empty and error states on individual screens, and a final contrast and
  TalkBack review are still to come.
