## Overview

This build is about goals. Every exercise now has a **Goals** tab where you can set targets and follow your
progress, with an optional goal line on its graph. Body measurements get goals too, with their history coloured by
whether each change moved you closer. You can also put measurements in your own order, record a measurement at a
chosen time, and give each exercise its own weight step and starting graph.

This update upgrades FitLens's database. Everything you've logged, imported or set up is kept. Backups from earlier
versions restore the same way.

## What's new

- **Exercise goals.** Open an exercise and choose **Goals** to add targets for:
  - max weight, estimated 1RM or max reps in a set;
  - volume in one set or in one workout;
  - for timed and cardio exercises, the longest set or the distance in one workout.

  Each goal shows a progress bar, your best so far with its date, and the day you reached the target. Goals can be
  edited, deleted and moved up or down. They follow your warm-up setting, and they're included in your backups.
- **Goal lines on graphs.** When an exercise has a goal that matches the graph you're viewing, a **Goal** chip draws
  the target as a line, in full screen too.
- **Body measurement goals.** On the Body tab, tap the goal line under the measurement chips to choose **Increase**,
  **Decrease** or a **specific value**. In the history, changes towards your goal show in gold and changes away from
  it in purple, and the + or − sign still tells you which way it went.
- **Your own measurement order.** **Reorder** on the Body tab sets the order of your measurements. Drag them, or use
  TalkBack's move actions.
- **Record the time.** Adding a measurement now takes a time as well as a date.
- **Per-exercise defaults.** The exercise editor can set a **weight step** for that exercise, used by the + and −
  buttons ahead of the step in Settings, and the graph it **opens on**.

## Improved

- **Your goals and order are kept.** Goals and the measurement order you set in FitLens are no longer replaced when
  you import a FitNotes backup again.

## Known limitations

- Measurements you've already recorded can't be edited yet. You can delete one and add it again.
- A default set of body measurements for new users, and unit conversion for measurements, are still to come.
- A default rest time per exercise will arrive with the rest timer.
