## Overview

Graphs now take the room they deserve. They're bigger, their controls fit on one compact line, their lines are never
broken, and Analysis shows its totals as a line. Switching between kilograms and pounds now converts every weight,
including body measurements such as bodyweight.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## What's new

- **Body measurements follow kg or lbs.** Bodyweight, and any other measurement in a weight unit, now shows in the
  unit chosen in Settings → Units & display: on the body tracker's track list, history, graph, stats and goals, the
  day log, the calendar, the PDF report and CSV exports. You enter and edit values in that unit too. What's stored is
  never rounded or changed, so switching back and forth is always safe.
- **Analysis totals as a line.** Analysis → Workouts shows your weekly, monthly or yearly totals as a line, which shows
  progression over time better than bars. It has a trend line, tap for details and full screen with zoom. A week,
  month or year still in progress is noted under the graph.

## Improved

- **Bigger graphs.** Graphs are sized to your screen, about half its height, instead of a fixed small size.
- **Compact graph controls.** The graph type, the rep count and the range are dropdowns. Trend line, Start from zero
  and the goal line are in a ⋮ menu beside them. Full screen sits on the same line. This applies on the exercise
  Graph tab, the body tracker, Analysis and in full screen. The body measurement and Analysis's measure, period and
  training filter are dropdowns too.
- **Unbroken lines.** A graph's line now joins every point, however far apart. It used to leave a gap after eight
  weeks without data.
- **The plate calculator follows kg or lbs.** A custom list of plates is converted when you change the unit.

## Known limitations

- The same compact controls are coming to the other screens in later updates (#115).
- Distances show "dist" as their unit until unit settings arrive (#7).
