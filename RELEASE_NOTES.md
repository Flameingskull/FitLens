## Overview

A small update that refreshes the FitLens icon. Nothing else in the app changes.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## Improved

- **A sharper FitLens icon.** The home-screen and app-drawer icon now uses the refreshed artwork: crisper gold
  outlines and a richer imperial purple. It still fits every launcher shape (circle, rounded square or square) without
  cropping. Some launchers take a minute to show the new icon after the update.

## Known limitations

- Distances show "dist" as their unit until unit settings arrive (#7).
