## Overview

Settings is now quicker to find your way around. A search field finds any setting, every page is built from the same
clear rows, and the rest timer has a Settings page of its own. On wide screens and unfolded foldables, the calendar
shows the selected day beside the month.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## What's new

- **Search settings.** Type in the field at the top of Settings, a setting's name or a related word such as "rpe",
  "pounds" or "vibrate", and every matching setting is listed with the page it's on. Tap one to go there.
- **Settings → Rest timer.** The rest length and what happens when a rest ends (start after saving a set, vibration,
  sound and volume) now have their own page. They were only reachable from the rest timer itself. Both places show
  the same options, so a change in one shows in the other.
- **The calendar on wide screens.** On an unfolded foldable, a tablet or a phone in landscape, the selected day's
  workout, body values and photos sit beside the month instead of below it.

## Improved

- **Clearer settings.** Every setting is a full-width row, and the whole row is the button. A setting with a few
  choices shows the current one in gold, and tapping it opens the choices in a sheet, each explained where it helps.
  For example, each estimated 1RM formula shows what it makes of your best recent set.
- **Easier with TalkBack.** Each setting is read as one sentence (its name, its current value or state, then what it
  does), and every row is a large touch target.

## Known limitations

- The Backups, FitNotes import and Data tools pages keep their current card layout for now (#86).
- Sharing from the calendar isn't available yet (#87).
- Distances show "dist" as their unit until unit settings arrive (#7).
