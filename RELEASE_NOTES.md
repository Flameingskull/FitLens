## Overview

This update carries the consistent wording from the last two releases into Analysis and the Body Tracker. Every
Analysis tab, the exercise Stats and Goals tabs, the 1RM calculator, the calendar filter, the Body Tracker and its
Measurements, goals and body fat calculator now take their text from one place in the app, ready for future
translations. Counts and times read properly throughout, for example "1 workout", "1 day ago" and "Open 1 workout".

There's no database change. Everything you've logged, your pinned graphs and your chosen chart types are kept.
Install it over the current app as usual.

## Improved

- **Analysis.** The Overview's pinned cards, Workouts (with its graph names, summary and change against the period
  before), Breakdown, Exercises, Goals and the Records board all use the same consistent wording. Counts read
  correctly for one or many, such as "1 set" or "12 reps" in the Breakdown.
- **Exercise Stats, Goals and the 1RM calculator.** Every tile, goal line, the goal editor, the Estimated 1RM
  Calculator and Estimated 1RM Settings use the same wording, including what TalkBack reads for each rep max.
- **Body Tracker.** Track, History and Graph, a single measurement's screen, Measurements, the log and goal sheets,
  custom metrics and the body fat calculator are all reworded consistently. "How long ago" reads correctly for one
  or many ("1 hour ago", "3 days ago").
- **Graphs everywhere.** The graph controls (range, chart type, trend line, start from zero, share), the full-screen
  viewer and its TalkBack zoom actions, and the names of every exercise graph now come from one place. Your pinned
  graphs, default graphs and chart types keep working exactly as before.
- **Days and dates.** Today, Yesterday and Tomorrow, the calendar's day filter and the day bar's labels use the same
  wording.

## Known limitations

- The photo screens (Photos, the viewer and the slideshow), first-run setup, the rest timer and its notification, and
  the PDF report's own headings still keep their own wording and will move over as they're next updated.
- The body fat calculator's measurement names and instructions (height, neck, waist, hips) come from the formula's own
  definitions and stay as they are for now.
- Shared screen transitions, the remaining empty and error states on individual screens, and a final contrast and
  TalkBack review are still to come.
