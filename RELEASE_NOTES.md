## Overview

Comments now do two clearly different jobs. The **workout comment** is your note on the day and the workout as a
whole. The **exercise comment** is for detailed notes on one exercise, such as form, feel, equipment or what to change, and
FitLens brings them back the next time you train it.

There are no database changes. Everything you've logged is kept as it was.

## Improved

- **Exercise comments are made for detailed notes.** The box is larger, and opening it lists your earlier notes on
  the same exercise, newest first, so you can build on them.
- **Last time's note, when you need it.** On the exercise screen's Track tab, under the exercise comment, FitLens
  shows the note from the last workout that had one, with its date.
- **The workout comment has its own heading** on the day log, under the exercises, so it reads apart from the
  exercises' notes. Its box asks for notes on the day and the workout as a whole.
- **The README** describes the FitNotes-style Body Tracker from 1.0.84.

## Known limitations

- Earlier notes show the five most recent; every note is still in the exercise's History.
