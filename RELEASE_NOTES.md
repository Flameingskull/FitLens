## Overview

This build brings **supersets**, as in FitNotes. Group two or more exercises and FitLens keeps them together on the
day log, marks them with a gold bar, and moves you from one to the next each time you save a set. Saved workouts and
routines can hold supersets too.

This update upgrades FitLens's database to remember supersets. Nothing you've logged changes, and backups from
earlier versions restore the same way.

## What's new

- **Make a superset.** On the day log, open an exercise card's menu and choose **Superset with…**, then tick the
  other exercises of the day to group with it. **Remove from superset** takes one out. A superset left with a single
  exercise ends by itself.
- **Supersets stay together.** Grouped exercises sit next to each other under a **Superset A** label with a gold bar,
  on the day log and in the workout drawer.
- **Round-robin logging.** On the exercise screen, saving a set of a superset exercise takes you straight to the next
  exercise in the group, and back to the first after the last. A gold line under the tabs shows the group and where
  you are. If the rest timer starts after each set, it now waits until the whole round is done.
- **Supersets in saved workouts.** In the workout editor, an exercise's menu has **Superset with the next exercise**
  and **Remove from superset**. Adding the workout, or a routine day that uses it, keeps its supersets, and **Save as
  a workout** keeps a day's supersets.

## Known limitations

- Supersets can't yet be made from the workout drawer. Use the exercise card's menu on the day log.
- Copying or moving a workout to another day doesn't carry its supersets.
