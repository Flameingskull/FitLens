## Overview

A branding update: the FitLens character now stands faintly behind every screen, and notifications show the
character from the waist up, which reads better at that small size. Nothing else in the app changes.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## Improved

- **The character behind every screen.** The full-body FitLens character is drawn softly behind the app, under all
  text, cards and buttons. It's kept dim and fades out towards the top and sides, so everything on screen stays as
  easy to read as before.
- **A clearer notification picture.** The rest timer, workout timer, "Rest over" and backup notifications now show
  the waist-up character, which is easier to make out at notification size than the close-up used in 1.0.60.

## Known limitations

- Distances show "dist" as their unit until unit settings arrive (#7).
