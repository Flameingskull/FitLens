## Overview

FitLens has its own icon, and its exercise analysis now matches FitNotes. Every exercise offers FitNotes's full graph
list, its Stats tab has FitNotes's tiles and a custom date range, and Analysis gains the Exercises and Goals tabs. A set
in an exercise's history now opens with one tap to be corrected or deleted.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## What's new

- **The FitLens icon.** The app now carries its own icon on your home screen: the gold-outlined athlete on imperial
  purple. It fits every launcher shape (circle, rounded square or square) without cropping.
- **More graphs for every weight exercise.** The Graph tab (and the new Analysis → Exercises) adds three of FitNotes's
  graphs:
  - **Max volume:** your best single set each day (weight × reps);
  - **Max weight for reps:** the heaviest set at a rep count you choose, from 1 to 15;
  - **Personal records:** each day you set a record, at the best estimated 1RM of your records so far. It's built from
    the same records as the trophies on your sets, so the two always agree.
- **Analysis → Exercises.** Choose any exercise and see its graphs with the same ranges, trend, from-zero, goal line and
  full-screen options as the exercise screen. Your choice is kept while the app is open.
- **Analysis → Goals.** Every exercise goal in one place, with its progress, grouped by exercise. Tap a goal to open
  that exercise's Goals tab, or tap **+** to add a goal for any exercise.
- **Custom date range on Stats.** Next to All, 1Y, 3M and 1M, **Custom** picks any range of dates. The chosen range
  shows on the chip.
- **Edit a set from History.** Tap any set on an exercise's History tab to open it on the exercise screen, ready to
  update or delete.

## Improved

- **Stats tiles follow FitNotes.** Max weight, Estimated 1RM, Max reps, Max volume, Workout reps and Workout volume,
  each with the date it happened, plus the longest workout for timed exercises. Tap any tile with a date to open that
  day on the exercise screen.
- **FitNotes's graph names.** "Est. 1RM", "Volume" and "Total reps" are now "Estimated 1RM", "Workout volume" and
  "Workout reps". An exercise's default graph still opens the same graph as before.
- **A Max volume goal** now shows as a line on the Max volume graph.

## Known limitations

- Timed and distance exercises don't yet have the Max distance, Max time, Max speed and Max pace graphs, or pace on
  their Stats tiles. Those need the distance unit setting (#7).
- Sharing a graph as an image isn't available yet (#22).
- The rep count chosen for "Max weight for reps" and the exercise chosen in Analysis → Exercises are kept until the
  app closes, not across restarts.
- Distances show "dist" as their unit until unit settings arrive (#7).
