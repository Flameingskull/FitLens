## Overview

A small branding update: the FitLens character now appears in your notifications and in every search field. Nothing
else in the app changes.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## Improved

- **The FitLens character in notifications.** The rest timer, the workout timer, the "Rest over" alert and automatic
  backup alerts show the character, in colour, beside the notification text. The small status-bar icon stays a
  simple symbol, because Android shows only its outline.
- **The character in search fields.** Searching the exercise library, any exercise or category picker, and
  Settings shows the character beside the magnifying glass.

## Known limitations

- Distances show "dist" as their unit until unit settings arrive (#7).
