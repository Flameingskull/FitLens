## Overview

This update brings five more FitLens screens in line with FitNotes. **Settings** is now FitNotes's single list, under
its SETTINGS, DATA and OTHER headings, with checkboxes for on/off settings. The day log's **Copy Workout**,
**Workout Time**, **Share Workout** and **Create Workout** dialogs follow FitNotes's layouts, and the **Estimated 1RM
Calculator** now reads exactly as FitNotes's does, with its **Estimated 1RM Settings** alongside. The **New exercise**
form is a full screen, and **Breakdown** gains FitNotes's date list and slice arrows.

There are no database changes. Everything you've logged is kept as it was, and so are all your settings.

## What's new

- **Estimated 1RM Settings.** Choose the most reps a set can have to count towards estimated one-rep maxes
  (FitNotes recommends 10 to 15). Open it from Settings or from the gear beside the calculator on an exercise's
  Records tab. Reset returns to the formula's own limit. Your logged sets never change; records, graphs and the
  calculators follow the new limit straight away.
- **Unit System.** One choice switches weight, distance and body measurements to metric or imperial together. The
  separate unit choices stay just below it for any mix you prefer.
- **Help, Feedback, Change Log, Privacy Policy and About** in Settings, as FitNotes has them. Help, Feedback and the
  change log open the FitLens pages on GitHub in your browser; FitLens itself still has no internet access.

## Improved

- **Settings is one list, as in FitNotes.** Theme, Unit System, Calendar Week Start, Default Weight Increment, Home
  Screen Settings, Track Personal Records, Mark Sets Complete, Auto-Select Next Set and Keep Screen On come first,
  in FitNotes's order and wording, followed by FitLens's own settings. DATA holds Backup, Restore, Automatic Backup,
  Spreadsheet Export, Calculate Personal Records, Delete Workout History and Import From FitNotes. On/off settings
  are checkboxes on the right. Search filters the list, and the settings change right there in the results.
- **Copy Workout** on the day log's ⋮ menu is one dialog with FitNotes's three choices: Copy This Workout, Move This
  Workout and Copy Previous Workout, each with a line of explanation.
- **Workout Time** shows the date with a ⋮ menu (clear the time, or end it now), then Start Time and End Time fields,
  Start Timer (or Stop Timer while it runs), and Close and Save. The duration still shows as it runs.
- **Share Workout** opens on FitNotes's checklist: Select All, each exercise in capitals with its own checkbox, then
  every set with a checkbox. Text or image, what to include and a progress photo are under Options.
- **Create Workout** (Create workout from this day, on the ⋮ menu) uses the same checklist, so you can leave sets out
  before saving the day as a workout. Edit saves it and opens the workout editor.
- **The Estimated 1RM Calculator** lists 1RM to 8RM as FitNotes does, each weight with its percentage of the 1RM
  under it, then FitLens's 9RM to 12RM and percentage table. The + and − buttons step by your weight increment.
- **New and edit exercise** is a full screen, as in FitNotes: Name, Notes, Category (with + for a new one), Type and
  Weight unit, then FitLens's own defaults. ✓ in the top bar saves; ✓+ saves and starts the next exercise in the
  same category.
- **Breakdown** picks its period from a Date list of every week, month or year that has training, newest first, and
  up and down arrows beside the chart step through the slices.

## Known limitations

- FitLens has one look, black and gold, so Theme has no other choices.
- Rate FitNotes, Supporter, Twitter and Beta Testing have no FitLens equivalent and aren't shown.
- Nothing has yet been checked on a 320dp-wide screen; please report anything that looks squeezed.
