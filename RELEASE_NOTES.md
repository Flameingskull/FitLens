## Overview

A branding update with clearer artwork. The FitLens character has been redrawn to read better at small sizes, and
this update uses the new artwork everywhere. Nothing else in the app changes.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## Improved

- **A clearer app icon.** The home-screen and app-drawer icon uses the new full-body artwork, with bolder gold
  outlines that stay clear at icon size. It still fits every launcher shape without cropping. Some launchers take a
  minute to show it after the update.
- **The new artwork behind every screen.** The faint character drawn behind the app is the new full-body art.
- **A clearer notification picture and search badge.** Notifications (rest timer, workout timer, "Rest over" and
  backup alerts) and the badge beside the magnifying glass in search fields now use the new torso artwork, which is
  easier to make out at those small sizes. The search badge keeps the artwork's own wide shape instead of a square.

## Known limitations

- The compact controls are coming to the other screens in later updates (#115).
- Distances show "dist" as their unit until unit settings arrive (#7).
