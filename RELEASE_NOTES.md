## Overview

This build completes FitLens's exercise details. You can choose the formula behind every estimated one-rep max. Two
new calculators on the exercise screen work out your sets and the plates to load. A new overview shows an exercise's
history, graph, records, stats and goals in a single sheet.

There's no database change in this update, and nothing you've logged changes when you install it.

## What's new

- **Choose your 1RM formula.** **Settings → Personal records → Estimated 1RM** offers **Automatic** (FitLens's blend
  by rep range, the default), **Epley**, **Brzycki**, **Lombardi**, **O'Conner** and **Wathan**. Each option shows
  what it makes of your best recent set, for example "100 kg × 8 → 124 kg", so you can compare them before choosing.
  Your choice applies everywhere an estimate appears: graphs, records, rep maxes, goals, the calculators and the PDF
  report. Your logged sets are never changed.
- **Set calculator.** On the exercise screen, open the ⋮ menu and choose **Set calculator**. Work from your one-rep
  max, with each percentage from 100% down to 50% and the reps it should allow, or build a warm-up ramp up to a
  target weight. Weights are rounded to the exercise's weight step, and **Use** fills in the set.
- **Plate calculator.** Choose **Plate calculator** from the same menu. Enter a target weight to see the plates for
  each side, drawn as the end of a bar. Set your bar weight, whether the bar counts towards the target, and the plates
  you have. FitLens remembers them, and they're included in backups. **Use** fills in the weight actually loaded.
- **Exercise overview.** Tap an exercise under the calendar's selected day, or choose **Overview** from an exercise's
  menu on the day log. One sheet shows its **History**, **Graph**, **Records**, **Stats** and **Goals**, and
  **Open full screen** takes you to the matching screen.

## Known limitations

- The plate list is kept in the weight unit it was entered in. If you switch between kg and lbs, update it in the
  plate calculator, or tap **Reset to standard plates**.
- The plate calculator assumes you have as many of each plate as you need.
- Each formula only estimates from sets within its valid rep range: Brzycki up to 10 reps, Epley, Lombardi and
  O'Conner up to 12, Wathan up to 15, and Automatic up to 20.
